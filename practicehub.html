Important Excel Formulas to Know

🔢 Math & Stats

=SUM(A1:A10) =AVERAGE(A1:A10) =COUNT(A1:A10) =MAX(A1:A10) / =MIN(A1:A10) =ROUND(A1,2)

Foundation formulas — used in almost every Excel task.

🔍 Lookup & Reference

=VLOOKUP(val,range,col,0) =XLOOKUP(val,lookup,return) =INDEX(range,MATCH(val,col,0)) =HLOOKUP(val,range,row,0)

Used to fetch data from tables. XLOOKUP is the modern VLOOKUP replacement.

🔀 Logical

=IF(condition,true,false) =IFS(c1,v1,c2,v2,...) =AND(cond1,cond2) =OR(cond1,cond2) =IFERROR(formula,"")

Control formula output based on conditions. IFERROR handles divide-by-zero gracefully.

📊 Conditional Aggregation

=SUMIF(range,criteria,sum_r) =SUMIFS(sum_r,r1,c1,r2,c2) =COUNTIF(range,criteria) =COUNTIFS(r1,c1,r2,c2) =AVERAGEIF(range,crit,avg_r)

Aggregate only rows that meet your conditions — like SQL WHERE.

📝 Text Functions

=LEFT(text,n) / =RIGHT(text,n) =MID(text,start,len) =LEN(text) =TRIM(text) =TEXTJOIN(",",TRUE,range)

Clean, split and combine text data from messy datasets.

📅 Date & Time

=TODAY() / =NOW() =DATEDIF(start,end,"D") =WORKDAY(start,days) =EOMONTH(date,0) =YEAR(date) / =MONTH(date)

Calculate deadlines, tenures, and date differences in reports.

💰 Financial

=PMT(rate,nper,pv) =NPV(rate,cashflows) =IRR(cashflows) =FV(rate,nper,pmt) =PV(rate,nper,pmt)

Essential for loan calculations, investment analysis and financial modelling.

🎯 Advanced Lookup

=FILTER(range,condition) =UNIQUE(range) =SORT(range,col,order) =SEQUENCE(rows,cols) =OFFSET(ref,rows,cols)

Dynamic array formulas (Excel 365+) — replace complex array formulas.
